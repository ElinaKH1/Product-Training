# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Experienced Delivery Driver
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Complete the assigned route accurately and on time, with fast and reliable access to essential information and actions.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Core actions take too many steps, while crashes, poor offline access, delayed updates, and unreliable uploads force the driver to use texts, screenshots, calls, and paper backups.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** WhatsApp or text messages, phone calls, screenshots, and printed copy of the delivery route.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Screenshot or print the route before leaving; manually override unsuitable directions; text delivery updates to the dispatcher; call the office if route information disappears; retake proof-of-delivery photos until the upload works.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The process feels most broken when the driver cannot access or trust the route during a delivery and has to stop working to contact the office.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** “No signal in half my area and the app is useless offline. I screenshot my route every morning as a backup.”
