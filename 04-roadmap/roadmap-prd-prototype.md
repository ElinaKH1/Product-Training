# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** Experienced delivery driver who relies on RouteLogic throughout the day to navigate routes and complete deliveries.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** Increase Driver Compliance CSAT from 2.8 to at least 3.5.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** Completing essential delivery and compliance tasks takes too many steps, pushing drivers to text dispatchers or use manual workarounds.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Maintain Manager Reporting CSAT at 4.5 or above, so simplification does not weaken valued reporting capabilities.

## Scan the backlog & set a human baseline
- **My instinctive “quick wins” before touching the AI (2 to 3 feature IDs + why):** B1, One-Click Compliance Checklist: directly addresses the high-friction compliance workflow and should improve the primary success metric.
 B6, Driver Alert Notifications: reduces reliance on texts and calls by making route changes visible immediately.
 B5, Step Progress Indicator: gives drivers clearer guidance through compliance tasks and reduces uncertainty about what remains.

## Audit, override & decide
- **Where did you override the AI? (feature + old vs. new score + why):** I did not make a major override. The scoring matched my assessment that B1 and B6 offer the strongest value. I kept B5 as a smaller supporting improvement, but below B1 because better progress visibility does not remove the underlying complexity.
- **Did the AI over-value a Sales/Eng request your M2 interviews don’t support?:** No. The Sales and Engineering requests were scored low because they do not directly address the driver’s validated compliance friction or move the primary metric.
- **Did it underweight something your M3 cohort/funnel data strongly supports?:** No major underweighting. The data strongly supports prioritizing B1 because Compliance has high driver usage but low satisfaction. B5 may help with workflow clarity, but it should not replace actual simplification.

## Generate your interactive roadmap
- **My “Now” lane (this sprint), the 2 to 3 quick wins I’ll build first:** B1 One-Click Compliance Checklist, B6 Driver Alert Notifications, and B5 Step Progress Indicator. Together, they simplify the highest-friction workflow, improve timely communication, and give drivers clearer progress.
- **What I cut, and the “no” I’m protecting the scope from:** I cut manager dashboards, AI ETA enhancements, reporting automation, audit exports, and coordinator-focused features. I am protecting the pilot from attractive requests that do not directly improve the driver’s compliance experience or primary metric.
